Best Global Brands 2026: a strong name is no longer enough

Interbrand's yearly ranking of the world's 100 most valuable brands is out. Apple stays on top, NVIDIA races into the top 10, and the report asks a new question for every brand designer: what happens when an AI agent, not a person, makes the choice?

The top of the table. Apple leads at $514.6 billion (up 9.3%), followed by Microsoft and Google, which climbed to number three with a 22.8% rise. NVIDIA grew its brand value by 71.5% to land at number six. Instagram sits at seven and YouTube at ten.

Who slipped. Coca-Cola grew only 0.4%. Toyota, still the top car brand at number nine, lost almost 20%. Tesla fell 25 places to number 50 and lost nearly 38% of its value. Interbrand calls this the car category's toughest year.

The big idea. The report's theme is that brands now speak to two audiences: people, and the AI agents that shop and compare for them. Interbrand says brands are not dying but facing "accelerated selection". Fewer names will truly drive choice.

What the winners did. Interbrand's CEO Gonzalo Brujó says winners use "brand" as "a business tool, not simply an asset". Hermès and Chanel grew in luxury, and new entrants include Zurich, Hitachi, GE Healthcare and Hewlett Packard Enterprise.

What this means for you

Design for two readers now. People still need a distinctive look they remember. AI agents need clear names, consistent product facts and clean structure they can read. In your next identity project, add a page to the guidelines on how the brand should appear in search results, AI answers and marketplace listings.

AI Desk

Runway · 26–27 Oct

Runway now makes ad variants and mini apps

Runway Ads reads your best-performing ads on Meta, Google and TikTok, creates on-brand versions for every placement and publishes the ones you approve. It comes with every paid plan. A day earlier, App Studio arrived: describe a creative tool in plain words and Runway builds and hosts it using its models and your brand files.

Higgsfield · 26 Oct

Higgsfield Playable turns a prompt into a game

Describe a game, or upload a gameplay video, and Playable builds a browser game in minutes: eight genres, 2D or 3D, with difficulty, enemies and colours changed by slider or text. The Enterprise version, Playable Ads, exports one HTML file of up to 5 MB for networks such as Meta, Unity Ads and Google App Campaigns.

Meta · 29 Oct

Meta quietly tests an AI stylist app

Style Sketch builds a virtual wardrobe from your Instagram photos, suggests outfits from clothes you own and shows them on a model matched to your body shape and skin tone. It also plans outfits for trips using the weather. For now it is only in Indonesia, Singapore and Thailand.

3D · 28 Oct

Maxon teases an AI tool and 3D artists push back

A short "coming soon" video, likely for Cinema 4D, drew angry replies on X. Some artists asked for something useful instead, like AI help with UV unwrapping. In March Maxon partnered with Tencent Cloud to bring its HY 3D engine, which makes base models from prompts or images, into Cinema 4D.

The Attar Letter Box

Diwali gifting season is here. Today's brief is a brand image: one hero flat-lay photo for "PATRA ATTAR CO.", an invented attar house from Kannauj that packs each perfume like a letter, with a wax seal and a postage stamp. AI loves to mirror letters, miscount objects and invent stamp text. Score your result before you use it.

Score: 0 / 5

Student task: keep the AI photo only for paper, light and bottles. Redraw the stamp and seal as vectors in Figma or Illustrator, set the Devanagari and English type yourself, then place them back on the photo with a soft shadow.

On warm cream handmade paper: a folded kraft envelope closed with a deep red wax seal stamped with one capital "P"; beside it a small postage stamp with perforated edges, a pink rose illustration, the word "गुलाब" and "₹5"; a plain card reading "PATRA · ATTAR OF ROSE · KANNAUJ"; exactly 3 small identical glass attar bottles with cork stoppers. Graphic Design

Type · 28 Oct

One magazine font grew into a museum's whole type system

London foundry A2-Type was asked for one serif for Denmark's Louisiana Museum of Modern Art's members' magazine. It ended up making four typefaces (Grotesk, Mono, Antikva and Plakat) and 80 matching pictograms. The team studied the museum's midcentury buildings and its full print archive, then tested the fonts on real surfaces at many sizes.

Packaging · 29 Oct

A perfume brand packed like a personal letter

Each Aplomb fragrance is inspired by a real person, so Studio Lotta Nieminen treated every perfume as a letter written to someone. The identity uses wax seals and an illustrated postage stamp for each scent. To stop it looking crafty, each stamp sits inside a debossed frame, balancing handmade warmth with luxury restraint.

Campaign · 29 Oct

Prada draws its holiday campaign by hand

"Drawing Prada" places the brand's ambassadors inside cartoon worlds drawn entirely by hand, with a sly fox, a goofy lynx and shoe-stealing mice. It was directed by John Pomeroy, who began at Disney in the 1970s. Creative Bloq contrasts it with the AI-made Christmas ads other giant brands have run.

UI / UX

Google · 29 Oct

Gemini's glowing new bar reaches every Android phone

The Gemini overlay now uses a heavy blur, tiny specks and swirls of colour, and a pill that turns see-through at the edges. It first came with the Pixel 11. The app can also now create and edit slide decks, sheets, docs and PDFs inside the chat, with export to PPTX or PDF.

Android · 29 Oct

Android 17 beta fixes bugs designers notice

Android 17 QPR3 Beta 3 brings back lock-screen blur and app lock, adds a camera Quick Settings tile, and screenshots now include the status and navigation bars. Its fix list includes images showing wrong transparency in Multiply blend mode, blank white buttons in dark mode and uneven status-bar icon spacing.

Forms · 28 Oct

Chrome autofill now pulls details from your Gmail

A "Suggested by Gemini" option can fill order numbers, tracking details and flight bookings found in Gmail. Users can delete single suggestions or switch the feature off in settings. It is rolling out in the US for Gemini in Chrome users with Personal Intelligence on, across Android, iOS and desktop.

Student Corner

Studio profile · 28 Oct

Start with a question, not an answer

Rio de Janeiro's Estudio Cru, run by Bernardo Winitskowski and Maria Alice Leal, leads every project with a concept and believes "more in the power of questions". Its work, from a Japanese bakery identity to a play-based festival, keeps the type clean and simple so the idea stays easy to read.

Talk · 29 Oct

Treat every brief as a way out of your comfort zone

At It's Nice That's Nicer Tuesdays in LA, The New Company's design director Will Geddes and producer Helena Corzan showed how the agency hunts for ideas that have not been done before, across work for brands like AG1, Nike and EA Sports. Their test for every brief: how do we find a new way through?

Design psychology term of the day

Mere-exposure effect

We tend to like things more simply because we have seen them many times. It is a big reason the world's top brands feel trustworthy. But today's ranking shows its limit: being familiar did not stop Coca-Cola's growth stalling at 0.4%.

Try it: pick one distinctive asset for your own brand (a colour, a shape, a type style) and repeat it in every post for a month. Then add one fresh idea each time so familiar never turns into boring.
