Lead story · Graphic Design · 26 Nov

A Glasgow studio builds a padel brand from its name to its rackets

Freytag Anderson created the brand for CCO, an Edinburgh padel startup, working from naming right through to the product.

What changed. CCO is a new premium padel brand. Freytag Anderson, a Glasgow studio that has won Scottish Design Agency of the Year four times, built the identity from the start, including the name and the rackets.

Why it matters. Padel is growing fast, mainly in North America and increasingly in the UK. That also means a crowded market of kit brands fighting for fans' attention, so a clear identity counts for more.

The lesson. A brand built from naming up to the product itself has one voice on court, in print and on the racket. Students can study how one studio carried the idea into physical objects.

What this means for you

Ask whether your brand idea can survive the product itself. Test a name, a logo and one physical object together before you call the identity done.

Student Corner

Term of the day

Serial position effect

People remember the first and last items in a list best. Put the key item at the start or end of a menu, nav bar or card set, not in the middle.

Student task

Build a six-item menu card for a chai café. Use a plain list. Ask three people to recall the items after 30 seconds, then move the most important item and test again.
