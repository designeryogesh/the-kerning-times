Lead story · AI · Entertainment · 9 Nov

Disney hires Adobe to build its private AI studio

On the eve of Adobe MAX, Adobe became Disney's "Official AI Innovation Partner". Adobe will build private Firefly models trained on Disney's own characters, worlds and art styles. For designers, it is the clearest sign yet that big brands want AI that draws only in their style.

The deal. The multi-year alliance was announced on 9 November in Miami Beach. No money or length was disclosed. Adobe will use Firefly Foundry to build private models for Disney's internal teams. They are not a public app. Disney also gets early access to Firefly, Photoshop and Premiere, plus a dedicated team of Adobe engineers.

Already in use. Since June, Walt Disney Imagineering has used Firefly Foundry tools to turn hand-drawn sketches into 2D concept art, make on-brand art for films like Frozen and Moana, and turn 2D renders into 3D prototypes. Disney says the tools are built on licensed and Disney-owned work. The aim is to cut months of work down to days or hours.

Why eyebrows are raised. Creative Bloq notes that Disney picked Adobe over OpenAI, Google and Anthropic, and that concept artists fear fewer jobs. Disney is planning cuts aimed at up to 20% annual savings, and July's layoffs included Pixar artists. Its $1 billion OpenAI deal from December 2025 ended when OpenAI shut Sora down about three months later.

The bigger shift. Adobe is moving from selling software to being an infrastructure partner that builds custom models for big IP owners. The deal also covers Walt Disney Studios' StudioLAB, ESPN Edge, the parks and Disney Cruise Line. MAX runs 10 to 12 November, and the opening keynote is at 7:30 pm IST today.

What this means for you

"AI that draws in our style" will soon be a normal client brief, from film studios to Indian FMCG brands. The model is only as good as what it learns from, so your value moves to defining the style: sketches, rules, references and taste. Keep a dated archive of your original process work, learn how a style guide becomes training data, and always ask who owns a model trained on your work.

ByteDance says Seedream 5.5 keeps untouched areas pixel-identical across up to 100 rounds of edits, with no colour shift. Video ads built from a chat

Higgsfield's Ads Studio now has an agent that builds video ads from an idea or a pasted script. You pick a style (micro drama, cartoon, cinematic, claymation), a product and an avatar. A brand kit can fill itself from your website link: colours, selling points, products.

Prompt of the day · Test it, score it, then redesign it

The Padwa Ledger Cover

On Padwa and the Gujarati New Year, many traders open new account books. Today's brief is a product mockup: a cloth-bound ledger for an invented stationery brand. Fabric texture, foil stamping and exact text on a curved spine test AI tools hard.

Score: 0 / 5

Student task: build the same cover in Photoshop with a real fabric texture and a foil-stamp layer style. Then write a one-page production spec: cloth, foil colour, board thickness and corner guards, as you would send to a printer.

Version 1 · Any tool
A product photo of one closed, cloth-bound account ledger for an invented stationery brand, KHATA & CO. Deep red linen cloth with visible weave, lying on a warm sandstone surface, top-down view tilted slightly. Gold foil stamping on the cover reads "New Page, New Year" in a serif, with "नवे पान" below it. Exactly three brass corner guards, one red satin ribbon bookmark coming out of the bottom, visible white stitching on the spine, "KHATA & CO." in small gold letters on the spine. Soft morning window light, one marigold flower beside it. No other text, no deities, no coins.

Why it works: it counts every object (3 guards, 1 ribbon, 1 flower) and names the material, so you can score texture and text separately.

Version 2 · Midjourney V8
product photography, one closed cloth-bound account ledger, deep red linen weave, gold foil stamped serif title, three brass corner guards, one red satin ribbon bookmark, white spine stitching, warm sandstone surface, single marigold, soft morning window light, slight top-down angle, premium stationery catalogue --ar 4:5 --style raw --no deities, coins, extra text, people

V8 tip: ask for "blank gold foil plaque" if the title comes out garbled, then set "New Page, New Year" and "नवे पान" yourself in Photoshop with a bevel and gold gradient.

Version 3 · Nano Banana
Step 1: Photograph one closed red linen cloth-bound ledger on a sandstone surface, slight top-down angle, three brass corner guards, one red ribbon bookmark, white spine stitching, soft window light. Blank cover.
Step 2 (same chat): Keep the book, light and angle exactly the same. Add gold foil stamping on the cover: "New Page, New Year" in a classic serif and "नवे पान" below it, slightly pressed into the cloth. Add "KHATA & CO." in small gold letters on the spine. Change nothing else.

What you are testing: whether the step-2 edit keeps the cloth weave and counts, and whether the foil looks pressed in rather than printed on top.

Version 4 · ChatGPT
Create a 4:5 product photo for "KHATA & CO.".
Layout:
1. Surface: warm sandstone, soft morning light from the left
2. Object: one closed ledger, deep red linen cloth, slight top-down angle
3. Cover text in gold foil: "New Page, New Year" / "नवे पान"
4. Details: exactly 3 brass corner guards, 1 red ribbon bookmark
5. Spine: white stitching, "KHATA & CO." in small gold letters
6. Prop: one marigold flower, lower right
No other text, no deities, no coins.

Tip: if the Marathi is wrong, reply "fix only the line नवे पान, same font size and foil, change nothing else" instead of starting over.

Graphic Design

Typography · Superior Type · 10 Nov

Heaven: one typeface from round to square

Petr Kučera's Brutalist sans comes in four families numbered 01, 03, 06 and 09 by roundness, not weight. A "SuperVariable" version slides between them on one axis while letter shapes stay the same. Each family has 18 styles, Latin and Cyrillic.

Posters · Poster House · 9 Nov

Soviet anti-drinking posters go on show

"Just Say Nyet" at New York's Poster House (12 Nov to 11 Apr) shows late-1980s posters from the USSR's anti-alcohol campaign. They swap the bold diagonals of constructivism for one simple central symbol, like a bottle or label. A good study in minimal visual metaphor.

Law · UPS · 2 Nov

UPS's famous shield is "not creative enough"

The US Copyright Office Review Board refused to register the UPS shield, calling it familiar shapes, conventional gradient shading and ordinary lettering. The effort behind a logo does not count, only the result. Trademark rights are not affected.

UI / UX

Google Messages · APK teardown · 9 Nov

Edit before you send, in your own editor

A teardown found an "Edit before you send" option for photos and videos in Google Messages. Instead of building an editor, Messages hands the file to your default editing app, like Snapseed, then takes it back. With no editor installed, it suggests one. Not announced yet.

Framer · Harness 2 · 3 Nov

Framer's rebuilt agent takes fewer steps

Framer says its rebuilt AI agent needs half the tool steps and 39% fewer cached input tokens per message. It can edit component fields, animations, code and SVGs. A new localisation panel lets you translate a site gradually, page by page.

Meta · Muse · 9 Nov

An AI agent made for the wrist

Meta's Muse app now runs on Apple Watch, with its character front and centre. You can text it, dictate or call, and a complication opens it fast. Designing an agent for the wrist means one action per screen and almost no reading.

Design psychology term of the day

Effort heuristic

People judge quality by how much effort they think went into something. It is why a hand-drawn fish game wins hearts over AI slop. But effort alone is not enough: the UPS ruling judged only the result.

Try it: add one process slide (sketches, rejected routes) to a portfolio project and ask three friends if it changes how they value the final work.
