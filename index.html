Lead story · AI · Software · Adobe MAX · 10 Nov

Adobe opens Photoshop to any AI agent

On day one of Adobe MAX in Miami Beach, Adobe rebuilt Firefly as an AI creative studio with its own agent, launched Photoshop 2027, and said outside AI agents will soon be able to work on your native Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and After Effects files. Your layers stay editable.

Bring your own agent. Adobe is adding support for MCP (Model Context Protocol), the open standard that lets AI agents use software tools. It is a preview for Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere and After Effects, with a private beta due in early 2027. Your chosen agent works on project files on your own computer, and layers, vectors, sequences and compositions stay editable. Creative Boom calls it one of the biggest shake-ups since Creative Cloud began in 2012.

Firefly becomes a studio. Instead of prompting for one image, you describe the result and Adobe's Creative Agent picks the tools and models. You can review its plan first, choose a model or leave it on auto, and step in at any time. Firefly now hosts models from OpenAI, ByteDance, ElevenLabs and Topaz, plus Kling 4.0, next to Adobe's own.

New tools to try. Firefly Design (beta) puts vector, image editing, 3D and print on one shared canvas. Firefly Graph (beta) chains tools and models into a saved, reusable workflow. Builder (private beta) turns a prompt into a clickable site or app prototype that Adobe says stays on your design system. Adobe also previewed "always-on agents" that keep recurring jobs, like weekly social posts, moving while you are away.

Photoshop 2027. Relight adds up to three draggable lights; Generative Restyle redraws an image in a new style with a strength slider. Both are layer-based. Remove now catches shadows and reflections, and Quick Compare shows before and after. Also coming: turning a flat flyer or PDF into editable layers, Generative Recompose for new aspect ratios, and Camera Angles. Pricing and credit use for agents were not given.

What this means for you

The tool is no longer the moat; your files and your judgement are. Name your layers, build clean templates and keep a written style guide, because agents will read them. Try Firefly Graph on one repeat job (like resizing a post into 12 formats), and spend the saved time on the idea, the type and the final polish that clients still pay a human for.

AI Desk

ArtStation · AI rules · 10 Nov

ArtStation hides AI art by default

Artists must now declare each upload as generative AI, mixed, or 100% non-generative. AI filtering is on by default, there is a channel to report unlabelled AI work, and viewers can set filters to Strict, Balanced or Loose. Critics worry false reports could hide human work.

Epic Games · Unreal Engine 5.9 · 10 Nov

A game engine built mostly by agents

Epic says the Unreal Engine 5.9 preview is its first release built mainly with agentic workflows, in about a week, for the MegaJam (12 to 15 Nov). New Unreal Canvas lets people and agents create text, images, video and meshes on one board. Developers are divided.

Adobe × Google · Partnership · 10 Nov

Near-real-time prompt edits in Photoshop?

Adobe and Google DeepMind are exploring a custom Google image model for Photoshop that would switch between prompt-based variations almost instantly. It is exploratory, with no date. Adobe tools are also coming to Google's AI Mode in Search and to Gemini.

Prompt of the day · Test it, score it, then redesign it

The Bhai Dooj Stamp

Today's brief is a commemorative postage stamp for Bhai Dooj by an invented stamp-art studio, TIKIT STUDIO. A stamp is tiny: perforated edges, a price, small bilingual text and a flat print look. All four are classic AI weak spots.

Score: 0 / 5

Student task: design a set of four Bhai Dooj stamps in Illustrator on one sheet, each with a different object (thali, sweet box, letter, rakhi-free gift), using the same grid, type and three colours. Print it at actual size (about 3 × 4 cm) and check if the text still reads.

Version 1 · Any tool
A single commemorative postage stamp for Bhai Dooj by an invented studio, TIKIT STUDIO, shown flat on plain white paper. Portrait format with even perforated edges on all four sides. Inside a cream border: a top-down tilak thali with exactly one lit diya (one flame), one small roli bowl and three laddoos, drawn as a flat 3-colour screen print in vermilion, marigold and cream. Text: "भाई दूज" large at the top, "Bhai Dooj 2026" small at the bottom, "₹5" in the top-right corner. No people, no hands, no deities, no other text.

Why it works: it fixes the format, counts every object and lists every word, so you can score edges, text and objects separately.

Version 2 · Midjourney V8
single postage stamp, portrait, even perforated edges, cream border, flat screen print illustration of a top-down brass tilak thali with one lit diya, one roli bowl, three laddoos, limited palette vermilion marigold cream, Indian folk graphic style, crisp vector shapes, stamp lying flat on white paper, philatelic catalogue photo --ar 3:4 --style raw --no people, hands, faces, deities, gradients, extra text

V8 tip: leave the text out of the prompt, then set "भाई दूज", "Bhai Dooj 2026" and "₹5" yourself in Illustrator so the Devanagari is perfect.

Version 3 · Nano Banana
Step 1: Make one portrait postage stamp with even perforated edges and a cream border, lying flat on white paper. Inside: a flat 3-colour screen-print of a top-down tilak thali with one lit diya, one roli bowl and three laddoos, in vermilion, marigold and cream. No text yet.
Step 2 (same chat): Keep the stamp, edges and artwork exactly the same. Add "भाई दूज" large at the top, "Bhai Dooj 2026" small at the bottom and "₹5" in the top-right corner, in cream-coloured bold sans. Change nothing else.

What you are testing: whether the step-2 edit keeps the perforations and the count of laddoos, and spells the Devanagari correctly at a small size.

Version 4 · ChatGPT
Create a 3:4 image of one postage stamp for "TIKIT STUDIO".
Layout:
1. Stamp: portrait, even perforated edges on all four sides, on white paper
2. Border: cream, about 6% of the width
3. Art: flat screen print, top-down tilak thali
4. Objects: exactly 1 lit diya (1 flame), 1 roli bowl, 3 laddoos
5. Colours: vermilion, marigold, cream only
6. Text: "भाई दूज" top, "Bhai Dooj 2026" bottom, "₹5" top-right
No people, hands, deities or postal logos.

Tip: if the teeth are uneven, reply "redraw only the perforated edge with equal round teeth, change nothing else" instead of starting over.

Graphic Design

Identity · Zap Energy × Manual · 10 Nov

One diagonal, from logo to lab door

Studio Manual's identity for US fusion-energy firm Zap Energy draws Z, A and P as heavy geometric letters, each cut by the same diagonal. That angle becomes moving stripes, patterns and even orange lab signs with room numbers and line drawings of equipment.

Promotion · Sitting Wren · 10 Nov

A publisher asks readers to "burn books"

Small publisher Sitting Wren made collectable matchboxes, proportioned like tiny books, with vintage bird art and punny lines. Creative director Ryan Hewlett wanted a promo people keep, not bin. The "burn" means a story burning into your mind.

Posters · HBO · 10 Nov

Why the Harry Potter posters feel off

HBO's new character posters split each face with a raised wand, and fans say the halves look stretched and lit by two different light sources. Fans quickly made better versions using just one half. Lesson: one light, one photo, one idea.

UI / UX

Accessibility · Apple TV · 10 Nov

Sign language, built into the player

The Apple TV app now offers on-screen ASL and BSL interpreters, in the bottom-right corner, designed and tested by Deaf and native signers. Supported titles get a Sign Language icon, and search understands "with ASL". It needs version 27.2 software.

WhatsApp · Android beta 2.26.45.5 · 11 Nov

WhatsApp's tab bar learns to float

Some beta testers now get a floating bottom bar with no text labels and a circular highlight on the selected tab. It shrinks into a small pill when you scroll down and grows back when you scroll up. It can hold five tabs.

Opinion · LS Digital · 10 Nov

Design for exposure, not age

LS Digital's D. Dhayan Kumar argues Indian apps now serve students, professionals and first-time users at once. A retired urban banker may be more confident than a young first-time user, so design for digital exposure and balance speed with reassurance.

Student Corner

Competition · Apple · 10 Nov

Swift Student Challenge 2027 announced

Apple will accept apps from 8 to 28 February 2027. Each app must be experienced within three minutes and, for the first time, can target any Apple platform. Judging covers innovation, creativity, social impact and inclusivity. Designers: team up with a coder friend now.

Career · Julien Gobled · 10 Nov

Work set hours, then let go

French designer Julien Gobled studied fine art, then worked agency and freelance jobs before going independent. He keeps a 9-to-6 routine even without a commission, and says he learned to stop controlling every step and let projects surprise him.

Design psychology term of the day

Locus of control

People are happier when they feel in charge of what happens. It is why Adobe keeps saying you stay "in the director's chair": plans you can review, layers you can edit, a way back to the full toolset.

Try it: add a preview or undo step to one flow in your next project, and ask three users if it makes them more willing to try the feature.
