Lead story · India · Tools

Figma's first Config India: "Design is the differentiator"

Figma held its first Config in India at BIEC, Bengaluru, on 15 October. More than 30,000 people signed up to attend in person or online. The launches were about AI agents, but the message on stage was about human judgement.

Why India. According to Dataquest, India is now Figma's second-largest market by active users. Its customers here include Airtel, Groww, Myntra, Swiggy, TCS and Zomato. The Bengaluru office opened about a year ago and is growing fast.

What is new for teams. Beyond the code tools covered earlier in The Kerning Times, Figma showed Guidelines (open beta), which help AI agents follow your design system, and a design system hub (preview) that keeps libraries, code sources and guidelines in one place.

Building, not just drawing. Figma Make now uses Payload, the open-source content tool Figma bought last year, so a prompted app can come with a database, file storage, login and an admin panel. Figma also added 20 new MCP partners, so agents in other tools can read Figma files. One of them is Bengaluru's Sarvam, called Figma's first Indian MCP partner.

The human part. CEO Dylan Field's advice, as reported by Dataquest: "Don't delegate your thinking to AI. You need to think." Rollout of the new features will happen over the coming weeks, and availability differs by plan.

What this means for you

Tools that turn designs into code are getting easier every month, so the skill that pays is deciding what to build and why. Start writing a short "spec" for every project: the problem, the user, three rules for the design, and what success looks like. Agents can follow it, and so can a hiring manager reading your case study. Students: Figma's Campus Leaders programme has just opened in India (see Student Corner).

Copyright · 18 Oct

Who owns a character made with seven prompts?

A US court case is testing whether an AI-made meme character, created with OpenAI's image tool, can be protected. Creative Bloq explains why it matters: if a few prompts are not enough human input, AI-made logos and mascots you hand to clients may be easy for rivals to copy.

Image · Price check

Nano Banana 2.1 cuts the cost of an image

Google's Nano Banana 2.1, out since 6 October, costs about US$0.034 per 1K image on the standard API, roughly half of version 2, says SmartScope. The older model is deprecated, but no shutdown date has been confirmed, despite a widely shared "29 October" claim.

Prompt of the day · Test it, score it, then redesign it

The Dussehra Sweet Box

In Maharashtra, people swap apta leaves on Dussehra as "gold". Today's brief is packaging: the lid of a square mithai gift box for an invented sweet shop. The hard parts for AI: the exact two-lobed apta leaf, a counted marigold toran, a true top-down view and a short line in Devanagari.

Score: 0 / 5

Student task: trace the best AI leaf in Illustrator as a clean vector, then rebuild the lid as a real dieline: 200 × 200 mm, 3 mm bleed, a Devanagari typeface you can name, and the brand in one colour. Print it, fold a mock-up box from card, and photograph it next to the AI version.

Version 1 · Any tool
Top-down flat photo of a square mithai gift box lid for "APTA & CO.", an invented sweet shop in Pune, India, made for Dussehra. Lid: deep maroon matte card with gold foil details. Along the top edge, a toran of exactly 9 orange marigolds evenly spaced on a thin gold thread. Centre: three green apta leaves, each with two rounded lobes joined at a notch, arranged in a small fan. Under the leaves, in Devanagari: "सोनं घ्या". Bottom centre, small serif caps: "APTA & CO.". Soft daylight, gentle shadow on a warm off-white table. No other text, no logo, no watermark.

Why it works: it counts the flowers, describes the leaf shape in plain words and fixes the camera angle. Counting, botany and flat views are all classic AI weak spots, so each one is easy to score.

Version 2 · Midjourney V8
top-down flat lay of a square Indian sweet gift box lid, deep maroon matte card with gold foil, a toran of nine orange marigolds on a gold thread along the top edge, three green two-lobed apta leaves in a small fan at the centre, Devanagari text "सोनं घ्या" under the leaves, small serif caps "APTA & CO." at the bottom, soft daylight, warm off-white table, packaging photography --ar 1:1 --raw --stylize 75

V8 tip: keep --stylize low so the model does not add extra flowers. If you get ten marigolds, note the fail rather than rerolling twenty times.

Version 3 · Nano Banana
Step 1: Create a square, top-down photo of a deep maroon matte gift box lid with gold foil edges on a warm off-white table. Along the top edge, a toran of exactly 9 orange marigolds on a gold thread. In the centre, three green apta leaves, each with two rounded lobes joined at a notch, in a small fan. No text.

Step 2 (same chat): Keep the box, flowers and leaves exactly the same. Add the Devanagari words "सोनं घ्या" under the leaves in gold, and "APTA & CO." in small serif caps at the bottom centre.

What you are testing: whether adding text makes the model redraw the art. Count the marigolds in step 1 and step 2. If the number changes, it fails test 4.

Graphic Design

Identity · 19 Oct

Arabic and English, side by side

Pentagram's Samar Maakaroun built the Design Doha Biennial identity as a flexible type system, not a fixed logo. Arabic and English share one thin, even stroke, and neither script sits above the other. Stretched strokes join letters like lines in a network. The biennial runs in Qatar from 5 November to 31 January.

India · Logo · 20 Oct

IMI brings three campuses under one logo

International Management Institute has replaced separate logos for its Delhi, Kolkata and Bhubaneswar campuses with one mark. It uses a double "I" for the institution and the individual, a hidden "M" for leadership, and blue and red for trust and ambition. A good example of a brand architecture clean-up.

Sport · Colour · 19 Oct

Purple may return to the Raptors

SportsLogos.Net reports a credible rumour that Toronto's NBA team will swap black for purple on its Statement uniform from 2027–28. Purple was the club's main colour at its 1995 launch. Not confirmed yet, but a reminder that a heritage colour can carry decades of fan memory.

UI / UX

Microcopy · 19 Oct

Play Store swaps percent for time left

In Play Store version 53.7, active downloads now show an estimated time remaining or the size left, instead of a percentage. A small change with a big lesson: tell users what they actually want to know. "2 min left" answers "can I wait?" better than "63%".

Accessibility · 19 Oct

Which Figma plugin checks contrast best?

A guide by Ben Walsh, picked by Smashing Magazine, compares contrast plugins. Stark is the best paid all-rounder; Perception and Able are lighter options (Walsh made Perception himself). Targets to remember: 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text and for icons and UI parts.

Reading pick · AI agents

Accessible design now helps bots too

Deque argues that AI agents read pages much like screen readers, through the accessibility tree. Clear labels, proper structure and predictable patterns help both. It cites WebAIM's 2026 finding that 95.9% of home pages had detectable accessibility failures. Fixing issues at the design stage is far cheaper.

Design psychology term of the day

Von Restorff effect

Also called the isolation effect: the one item that looks different from the rest is the one we remember. One orange dot in a black masthead, one bright button on a quiet screen.

Try it: on your next poster, pick only one element to break the pattern, by colour, size or shape. If two things try to stand out, neither does.
